Mac

Which download

Apple menu > About This Mac > Chip. "Apple M1", "M2", "M3" and so on: OdinsGate-mac-arm64.dmg. "Intel Core": OdinsGate-mac-x64.dmg. The Apple-chip build doesn't open on an Intel Mac, and the Intel build runs slowly on an Apple chip.

Install

  1. Open the disk image and drag Odin's Gate to the Applications folder shown next to it. Eject the disk image.
  2. Open Odin's Gate from Applications. macOS says it "could not verify" the app, because it isn't signed with a paid certificate. Press Done.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and press Open Anyway next to the line about Odin's Gate. Confirm with your password, then Open. macOS asks only this once.

On macOS 13 or 14 a right-click on the app and Open, then Open again, does the same.

If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", the download got a quarantine flag the app can't shed. Open Terminal (Spotlight, type Terminal) and run

xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OdinsGate.app

then open the app again.

Play

Press Play. The first start downloads the game client, about a minute; after that the game is up in seconds. Log in with a name and password you make up for this server, never the login of any other game or account; the first login with a name claims it.

Updates

The launcher updates itself when Sam publishes a new version. If it ever says "Please download the new installer", come back to the download page, download the disk image again and replace the app in Applications.

Where its files are, and uninstalling

The app is /Applications/OdinsGate.app; its settings and the game client are under ~/Library/Application Support/OdinsGate. To remove it, drag the app to the Trash and delete that folder.

Problems

Press Copy problem report in the launcher and paste it to Sam. It holds the launcher's log and what it found out about the server, nothing from the rest of your Mac.
